# Academic Writing Portfolio

This repository contains a collection of academic papers written for university seminars, coursework, and thesis research. These papers reflect my academic interests and my development as a researcher.

## Papers

### Seminar Papers
- **[The quintic Ornstein-Uhlenbeck volatility model for joint SPX-VIX calibration](seminar-papers/Quintic-Ornstein-Uhlenbeck-Volatility-Model.pdf)** — *Seminar activity, 2026.* Presented the theoretical foundations of the joint SPX–VIX calibration problem, covering implied volatility, VIX construction, and stochastic volatility modelling. Analysed the quintic Ornstein–Uhlenbeck volatility model, focusing on VIX and SPX derivative pricing, forward variance curve parametrisation, calibration methodology, and calibration errors.
- **[Feynman-Kac formula to solve Schr\"odinger’s equation and the derivation of the path integral](seminar-papers/feynmankac_schrodinger_pathintegral.pdf)** — *Exam of Galilean course, 2026.* Developed the theoretical framework of the Feynman-Kac formula, establishing the connection between stochastic calculus, parabolic PDEs, and the Schr\"odinger equation, and exploring its relation to Feynman path integrals. Applied the Feynman-Kac formula to the Black-Scholes model, deriving the European option pricing formula by representing the solution of the Black-Scholes PDE as an expectation under geometric Brownian motion.

### Thesis
- **[Mean field games with common noise: modeling systemic financial decarbonization](thesis/MeanFieldGames_commonNoise_thesis.pdf)** — *Bachelor's Thesis, 2025.* Studied the application of Mean Field Games theory in a common-noise setting to model companies' optimal emissions decisions under incentives favoring environmentally sustainable strategies. Conducted an in-depth review and rigorous correction of theorem proofs in an unpublished research paper, strengthening the mathematical validity of the theoretical results.
